Cylab Cyber Autonomy Initiative

Creating the scientific foundations of autonomous cyber systems will require a new, interdisciplinary, systems-thinking approach.

This initiative is our stake in the ground as cybersecurity enters a new era of agent-driven autonomous operations. We aim to advance the science of autonomous network security and give researchers, policy makers, and AI and security system builders the rigorous evidence they need to assess the capabilities, limitations, risks, and real-world implications of these emerging systems.

You can read more about our research vision and interconnected research thrusts here. We invite you to learn more about our ongoing work by exploring our open-source projects on GitHub.
