Cylab Cyber Autonomy Initiative

Creating the scientific foundations of autonomous cyber systems will require a new, interdisciplinary, systems-thinking approach.

We're a research group in CMU CyLab studying autonomous agents as cyber operators: how LLM-driven systems behave when given a network, a goal, and the tools to pursue it, on both the offense and the defense. Our work centers on building the benchmarks and evaluation harnesses needed to measure that behavior rigorously. Write-ups from that work are collected here; code and datasets are on GitHub.

We ran three of the latest open source models on our cyber ranges (Kimi K3, Qwen 3.8 Max, and GLM 5.2) and found that they are exceedingly capable at executing multi-stage network attacks, and are completely willing to do so.
